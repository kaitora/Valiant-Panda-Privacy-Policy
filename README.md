Privacy Policy for ValiantPanda Apps
Last updated: 08/05/2026

ValiantPanda (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates mobile applications available on the Google Play Store. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our apps handle your information.

1. Information We Collect
We do not collect, store, or process any personal data from users.

Our apps:

Do not require you to create an account

Do not collect personal information

Do not transmit data to our servers or any third parties

Do not use analytics, tracking tools, or advertising identifiers

2. Data Stored on Your Device
All data created within our apps is stored locally on your device.
We do not have access to this data.

If you uninstall the app, the data stored on your device is deleted automatically.

3. Third‑Party Services
Our apps do not use third‑party services that collect user data.

If this changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

4. Children’s Privacy
Our apps do not collect any personal information from users of any age, including children.

5. Security
Because we do not collect or store any personal data, there is no risk of your information being accessed, shared, or misused by us.
